## Governing equations and retained numerical cases

The base-MATLAB model makes these operations visible:

- Relative power: `10*log10(P/P_ref)` dB.
- Relative RMS voltage: `20*log10(V/V_ref)` dB.
- Power from voltage and real resistance: `P = V_rms^2/R`.
- Unequal-resistance correction: `10*log10(R_ref/R)` dB.
- Absolute references: dBm to 1 mW, dBW to 1 W, and dBV to 1 V RMS.

Retained expected cases include 2x power = `+3.010299956639812 dB`, 2x RMS voltage =
`+6.020599913279624 dB`, and equal RMS voltages at 75 versus 50 ohms =
`-1.760912590556813 dB` in power. Python's `math.log10` independently reproduced those
constants. MATLAB did not execute them here.
